# Working Agreement

Before making changes, read `docs/PRODUCT.md`, `docs/PLAN.md`, and `docs/STATUS.md`.

- Preserve the documented stack and product direction. If either must change, document the decision in the relevant files before implementation.
- Reuse and extend existing code before introducing a new component, abstraction, dependency, or system.
- Prefer the smallest workable implementation. Avoid unnecessary architecture and speculative features.
- Make one meaningful change at a time and verify it before moving on.
- Keep the product private, single-user, and personal; do not introduce SaaS, team, admin, or billing concepts.
- Maintain the dark, polished desktop experience, accessible interaction, and subtle motion described in `docs/PRODUCT.md`.
- After meaningful work, update `docs/STATUS.md` with the actual repo state, issues, decisions, and next exact task.
- If the build sequence changes, update `docs/PLAN.md` and keep one step clearly marked `CURRENT`.
